TWO PLAYERS / INTRODUCTORY BASE GAME

At the table.

Build a refinery, buy crude oil, refine it, and sell it. The richest refinery after three years wins.

Start together

Create a private table and share the invite. Each player places five tanks and clicks Save & lock. Click Start the game once both setups are locked. The same-device option lets you control both players from one screen.

Pick and place pipes

  1. Click a government pipe to choose your worker’s tile. Add its edge-sharing neighbors if you want more pipes. Or choose two or four pipes from a shop.
  2. In the action panel, select a pipe, rotate it, and click a grid square. New tiles must connect to an existing pipe edge; colors need not match.
  3. Place every selected tile, then confirm the purchase. You can cancel or clear the draft before confirming.

Machines cover one half of a pipe tile and split the lines underneath them. Tanks are assigned permanently to the grade you choose.

Take a turn

Choose a main action. Central actions have an optional paired secondary action, costing $10 before the action’s purchases. Government purchases and running your own pipes do not have secondary actions. Human Resources upgrades change these options.

After work, you may run all eligible machine-connected pipelines for $15 total. Click End turn when finished. There are eight rounds in year one, six in year two, and four in year three.

Buy, refine and sell

Click occupied market slots to buy oil. Click empty slots to sell your own oil. Complete sales before purchases. Your tanks must hold all oil at the end of the transaction.

To refine manually, click a tile in your refinery. Choose oil for the eligible pipelines passing through it and its destination grade. Refinement costs are cumulative across grades. Runs happen simultaneously, and only the final grades need tank capacity.

Contracts and orders

Acquire up to one contract per row with the Contracts & Loans action. Contracts may be active now or deferred until next year, except in the final year. The same action offers one $15 loan for a penalty.

Click your active contracts or a public order to deliver oil between actions. Contracts can be filled partially; orders must be filled completely at once. Higher grades can meet lower requirements at the requirement’s payment. Incomplete annual contracts are lost and add penalties.

Your game’s upgrades and valuations

This introductory game uses Government, Engineering, Human Resources, Refined Markets and Shops. Buy up to two available upgrades per upgrade action; purchased families lock until the next year. Immediate benefits appear as choices in the action panel. Annual benefits arrive on your first turn of the new year.

Final scoring includes cash, refined oil, pipelines, valuations 1/2/3 and the machine-pipeline tile, minus penalties. The scoring table shows each contribution. Ties go to the player ahead in turn order.

Leave and return

Your seat remains reserved when disconnected. Rejoin from the same browser or use your private recovery file on another device. Every confirmed move is saved and encrypted recovery snapshots are kept in the browser. Download a recovery file before clearing browser data, and keep it private.

The online host must keep the same backup key for recovery to work. Free hosting can lose its server files; browser backups are a recovery option, not an external database.
